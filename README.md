## 🔬 Research & Engineering Projects

### 1. Hypoxify | Biomedical AI & Medical Imaging
**Founder & Lead Researcher | 2026 – Present**

Developing computational and hardware-based approaches to biomedical imaging, cancer research, and diagnostic innovation.

- **Hypoxify MATA:** Designing a low-cost, non-invasive thermoacoustic imaging ring array to investigate deep-tissue pancreatic tumor hypoxia and cancer aggressiveness.
- **Hypoxify Annotation Suite:** Developing a physics-guided biomedical image segmentation platform adapting Segment Anything Model (SAM) architecture to challenging medical imaging data.
- **Research Mentorship:** Conducting research under Dr. Elise Fear, NSERC Chair in Microwave Imaging at the University of Calgary.

### 2. Operation Oracle | Multimodal Medical Diagnostics
**Lead Researcher & Inventor | 2025 – 2026**

An interdisciplinary medical diagnostic framework integrating artificial intelligence, microwave engineering, acoustic analysis, and computer vision.

**THORACIS AI — Multimodal Lung Screening**
- Explores microwave signal modeling and acoustic analysis for non-invasive pulmonary disease screening.
- Integrates electromagnetic simulation, antenna engineering, and machine learning.
- Investigates tissue-dependent dielectric properties and diagnostic signal characteristics.

**NOMA AI — AI-Powered Skin Lesion Classification**
- Developed a multi-class deep learning model for classifying benign and potentially malignant skin lesions.
- Implemented MobileNetV3-based classification and Grad-CAM explainability approaches.
- Published an open-source dermatological dataset and model bias analysis examining representation disparities across skin types.

**Recognition:** CWSF 2026 Silver Medal, National Finalist, CAYIA Outstanding Award, and Excellence in Healthcare Award.
