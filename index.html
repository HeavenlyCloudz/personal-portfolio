About DetectED

Bridging the gap between STEM education and healthcare innovation through youth-led action.

DetectED is a youth-led nonprofit organization I founded to advance health technology education, disease detection literacy, and biomedical innovation among students. Through hands-on workshops, educational kits, mentorship programs, and community partnerships, DetectED empowers youth to explore how engineering, AI, and healthcare intersect to address real-world medical challenges.

Our mission is to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and opportunities needed to understand, design, and communicate the next generation of health technologies. We envision a future where students are not just consumers of medical technology, but contributors to healthcare innovation, scientific discovery, and equitable disease detection solutions.

To date, DetectED has secured a $3,000 AIFY Community Impact Grant (Canada Service Corps), delivered the Health Innovation Summer Series (August 2026) to 60+ registered Black and racialized youth in partnership with the Power of Early Vision Foundation and ZCUSA, built clinical partnerships with 5+ organizations including the Alberta Children's Hospital, and scaled to a William Aberhart STEM Club chapter with 25+ active members.

Where I contribute

Physics-informed AI, medical device prototyping, and translational health research.

Hypoxify Labs

June 2026 – Present · Lead Researcher & Inventor

Designed Hypoxify MATA, a low-cost ($1,000 USD) non-invasive wearable thermoacoustic ring array powered by Raspberry Pi 5 to map deep-tissue pancreatic tumor hypoxia and stratify cancer aggressiveness. Built the Hypoxify Annotation Suite (physics-guided SAM adaptation), cutting manual clinical annotation from 30+ min to <10 sec per volume. Mentored by Dr. Elise Fear, NSERC Chair in Microwave Imaging, University of Calgary.

THORACIS AI · Operation Oracle

2025 – 2026 · Independent Researcher

Designed a low-cost multimodal lung screening device integrating microwave signal modeling and acoustic analysis for early-stage pulmonary disease tracking. Modeled compact Vivaldi antenna arrays against dielectric properties of healthy vs. diseased lung tissue using dynamic respiratory models and ex vivo tissue phantoms. Earned CAYIA Outstanding Award ($5,000), CWSF Silver Medal & National Finalist, and CYSF Gold Medal + Pfizer Oncology Special Award.

NOMA AI · Operation Oracle

2025 – 2026 · Independent Researcher

Trained a multi-class deep learning model on self-curated dermatological datasets to differentiate malignant and benign lesions, with a point-of-care diagnostic prototype and bias-mitigation methods. Published an open-source Kaggle dataset and model bias report (1,700+ views, 250+ downloads) analyzing discrepancies across racialized skin types.
